Leo local Privacy Policy

Leo local is a personal desktop application used only by its owner. With the owner's consent, it uses the Gmail API and the gmail.readonly permission to read email for local processing.

The app does not send, edit, or delete email. Gmail data is processed on the owner's computer. Any local copies stay on that computer until the owner deletes them. The app does not upload Gmail data to external servers or share it with third parties.

The owner can revoke the app's access in Google Account settings and delete any local copies from the computer.

Leo local's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
